# Комерційна пропозиція and звіт — structures

## 1. Комерційна пропозиція (КП)
Goal: the reader can say yes in one reading. One page for a single product or service; 2–4 pages for a project with options. DOCX or PDF; a pitch to many people → a deck (docs-pptx).

### Structure
1. Header: company logo/name, «Комерційна пропозиція № [номер] від [дата]», addressee («для ТОВ «…», [посада, ім’я]»).
2. One-sentence summary of what the client gets and the main number: «Запуск інтернет-магазину за 6 тижнів за 180 000 грн без ПДВ».
3. The client’s task in their words (2–3 lines) — proves you listened.
4. Solution: scope as a list of deliverables, not activities («каталог на 500 товарів з фільтрами», not «розробка каталогу»).
5. Price table: № · Позиція · Кількість · Ціна · Сума; totals; «з ПДВ / без ПДВ» stated once and clearly; options as separate columns or tables («Базовий / Оптимальний / Повний») — at most 3.
6. Terms: timeline with milestones, payment schedule (e.g. 50/50), what the client provides, what is not included.
7. Proof: 1–3 relevant cases with numbers and a link; only real, verifiable cases — never invent results or testimonials.
8. Validity: «Пропозиція дійсна до [дата]».
9. Next step and contact: one action («Підтвердьте лист-відповіддю — надішлемо договір і рахунок»), name, phone, email.

### Rules
- Amount figures come from a spreadsheet or script; totals with words only if the КП is also used as a basis for payment.
- A proposal addressed to a specific person that contains all essential terms and shows intent to be bound may be treated as an offer (ЦК ст. 641, `legal-basis.md` row 5). If the client does not want that, the wording of a disclaimer is a perfect-legal / lawyer question — do not invent one.
- No superlatives without proof («найкращі», «№ 1»); no fake urgency.
- Brand: logo and colours from the client; none given → neutral layout (docs-docx defaults). Visual identity work → perfect-brand.

## 2. Звіт (management or project report)
Goal: a decision-maker gets the conclusion on page 1 and the evidence after it.

### Structure
1. Title page: report name, period («за III квартал 2026 р.»), organisation, author, date. Internal reports may skip it — put the same data in a header block.
2. **Резюме** (≤ ½ page): the main conclusion, 3–5 key numbers with comparison (vs plan, vs last period), the decision or action requested.
3. Sections in order of importance, each opening with its finding as the heading («Витрати на доставку зросли на 18 % через тарифи перевізника»), then evidence: table or chart, 1–2 paragraphs.
4. Risks and open questions with owners and dates.
5. Appendices: full tables, methodology, sources.

### Rules
- Every number has a period, a unit and a source; derived numbers (%, growth) recomputed by the XLSX or a script, not typed.
- Tables: «Таблиця 1 — Назва» above the table; figures: «Рисунок 1 — Назва» below the figure; numbered in order and referenced in the text before they appear.
- Charts follow docs-xlsx §6 (one message per chart, axis from zero for bars, units in the title).
- Headings use Word styles (Heading 1–3) so the table of contents and PDF bookmarks build automatically; TOC only above ~8 pages.
- Long reports: page numbers, running header with the report name, landscape sections only for wide tables.
- Data from the user’s files is quoted as is; gaps are flagged («дані за серпень відсутні»), never filled with estimates without saying so.
